# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **PassGuard** project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behaviors

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive and constructive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other community members.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and technical experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project, security quality, and the overall developer community.
- Acknowledging and prioritizing technical merits and project goals rather than individual personalities.

### Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Public or private intimidation, stalking, or threats.
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical or electronic addresses, without explicit permission (doxxing).
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind in project spaces.
- Trolling, insulting, or dismissive comments.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate, unsafe, or hostile in a professional open-source environment.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code edits, issue reports, pull requests, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official project spaces—including GitHub repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project communication channels—as well as when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting Process

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers through the project's official repository contact mechanisms, such as GitHub Private Security Advisories or maintainer contact links designated on the repository homepage.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

---

## Consequences

If maintainers determine that a violation of this Code of Conduct has occurred, they may take appropriate and proportionate action, including:

1. **Correction / Warning**: A private or public explanation clarifying the violation and reinforcing community expectations.
2. **Temporary Restriction**: A temporary ban or restriction from interacting with project spaces or repository issues.
3. **Permanent Removal**: A permanent ban from participating in project repositories, issues, discussions, and community spaces.
